Sources

Every source used on this site, with its link and the date it was checked: 25 Nov 2026.

How the checking was done

  • Pages were read through a summarising fetch tool, so wording is paraphrase unless in quotation marks.
  • Where a point comes only from a search-result summary, the page is marked "search summary only" here and next to the claim. Treat those as leads to confirm, not settled facts.
  • Pages that refused our fetcher (Nominet's public pages, Namecheap, some others) are named in the text where they matter.
  • No account was opened, nothing was bought and no API that needs an account was called. No affiliate or referral links are used anywhere.
