Short answers

Can you buy a domain straight from the registry?
The sources show registries working through accredited registrars: ICANN describes one registry in charge of each top-level domain, with registrars paying it a per-domain fee and free to charge what they wish. Verisign says it manages relationships with about 3,000 accredited registrars. For .uk, Nominet requires every registrar to be a member. Whether a registry also sells to the public is not stated on the pages we could read for Nominet, EURid and .PT. Source: ICANN, what does ICANN do (older page); Verisign, the registry; Nominet, .uk registry FAQ. Checked 25 Nov 2026.
Do you need a broker?
Only when the name you want is already registered and the holder will not sell through a normal route. For a free name, no: you register it through a registrar. See brokers and the aftermarket.
What does a .com cost at the bottom of the chain?
Verisign's wholesale fee rose from $10.26 to $10.97 per domain-year on 1 November 2026, and ICANN's transaction fee is $0.20 per domain-year. Registrar margin sits on top and is set by each registrar. Source: Verisign Form 10-Q, quarter to 30 Sep 2026 (SEC); ICANN, registrar fee letter for fiscal year 2027 (PDF). Checked 25 Nov 2026.
