Extensions

Extension support for AWS Route 53 Domains
.ukyes (.uk, .co.uk, .me.uk, .org.uk) source

Open to the public, no restrictions. Transfer in by setting the Nominet IPS tag to GANDI (no auth code); request transfer within five days. Registrar associate is Gandi. Privacy: the TLD page says all information hidden, but the transfer guide says to enable privacy for .uk family you must open a support case. Deletion not possible, only let expire. No registration extension on transfer for .uk/.co.uk.

.euyes source

Requires a postal address in one of the 30 EEA states, or EU citizenship (specify country). Privacy protection not supported. Transfer lock not supported. Auth code required (also via my.eurid.eu). Domain deleted on expiry date; restoration up to 40 days.

.ptno (not in the supported TLD list) source

Geographic list for Europe has no .pt entry.

Privacy, security and the rest

Other details for AWS Route 53 Domains
Pricing modelnot stated
whyNot stated on pages read. Prices are per TLD in a PDF price list; premium domains priced by registry.
WHOIS privacyEnabled by default on registration and transfer, no separate charge mentioned. Not available for all TLDs. Note: Amazon Registrar hides all contact info; Gandi (registrar associate for some TLDs) hides all except organisation name. .eu not supported; .uk family needs a support case to enable. source
DNSSECSupported for registration of .com, .uk, .eu and others: you add the public key (KSK/ZSK, base64) via console or API; up to 13 keys for .com/.net, 4 for others. Route 53 can also sign hosted zones. No automatic DS publication described on the page read: you add keys yourself. source
Account securitynot stated
Bulk and resellernot stated
whyNo reseller programme found. Default quota 20 domains per account (increase on request); console transfer-in handles up to five domains at a time.
Other notesDomains are registered via Amazon Registrar or its associate Gandi depending on TLD. Transfer-in: 60-day rule, unlock, no pendingTransfer or similar statuses, disable DNSSEC at old registrar and wait 24 hours. Owner contact changes are ignored during ccTLD transfers. Most gTLD transfers add a year; .uk, .co.uk, .me.uk, .org.uk do not. Domain registered in us-east-1 region only. Domains usually deleted on account closure. source

Flags: conflicts or doubts

  • Privacy for .uk: the TLD page says 'All information is hidden' while the transfer guide says a support case is needed to enable privacy for .uk, .co.uk, .me.uk, .org.uk.
  • Some Route 53 .uk docs say the IPS tag is GANDI even though AWS runs the console; this reflects the registrar associate.
  • Auth style for the API was not read on the pages fetched; the IAM/SigV4 statement is general knowledge and should be confirmed.
  • No prices captured: the price PDF could not be read.
