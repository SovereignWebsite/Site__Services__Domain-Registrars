Registrars with APIs

16 registrars, read from their own public pages on 25 Nov 2026. Every cell is either sourced (with a link) or says not stated. No account was opened, nothing was bought and no API call needing an account was made.

Prices are shown exactly as each vendor displayed them on that date, in its currency, and may be promotional or exclude tax. They are strings, not converted. Click "how to read" for the note on each. Machine-readable: registrars.json (schema).

How to read the prices. Every price was checked on 25 Nov 2026 and is shown in the vendor's own currency. First year and renewal are different numbers, so compare both, and the transfer price too. VAT or sales tax is not included unless the cell says so. An empty cell means not stated, not free. Prices change, and many first-year prices are promotions: check the vendor before you buy.

Our reading, not a sourced fact. Pricing model, in a sentence: Cloudflare states it charges at cost; other vendors in the data show retail margin, volume tiers, or do not say. Compare renewal against first-year price, which often differs.
